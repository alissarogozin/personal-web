processDescription: "Three phases: research the gap, structure the inventory, and build the system sellers actually use."
processIntro: "Phase 1, Research Gathering, meant interviewing Partner Account Managers and partner leaders to separate AI use cases with genuine field investment and dedicated sellers behind them from what partners were merely marketing. Phase 2, Use Case Inventory Structuring, organized that validated data into a comprehensive inventory mapping partner capabilities, certified hardware, and GTM strategies directly to the RHAIE and RHAIFN portfolios, then stress-tested it against a real, high-stakes vertical: fraud detection in Financial Services. What came out of those two phases was a three-part system: the Use Case Inventory as the validated, structured source of truth; a NotebookLM Knowledge Assistant sellers query in natural language, like \"How is Cisco targeting fraud detection?\", for real-time, grounded answers; and a closed feedback loop, where sellers feed newly discovered use cases back into the inventory, keeping it current without a central bottleneck."
