processDescription: "From a shared brainstorm to five core features and a mascot built to feel approachable, not enterprise."
processIntro: "All of this pointed to one guiding question: how might we create equitable learning opportunities for elementary school students across different income levels in Boston by creating technology that encourages personalized learning? Early concepts included a video-conferencing interface, an offline-capable activity library, gamified prompts, and structured buddy-getting-to-know-you activities. We looked at Zoom, Google Meet, and PBS Kids for interface inspiration, since we wanted something playful and familiar rather than enterprise classroom software."
iterations:
  - title: "Five core features"

  - title: "Branding: naming and testing mascots"
    caption: "We named the product Eddy, combining 'education' with a mascot we could personify, and tested a fox, bear, frog, rabbit, cat, husky, and panda before landing on the fox, then built a full type system, button styles, and color palette."

  - title: "Safety built in, not bolted on"
