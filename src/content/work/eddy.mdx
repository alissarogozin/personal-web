  - title: "Designed for real school resourcing, not just 1:1 devices"
    body: "Only about half of U.S. school districts have 1:1 device programs. For schools without one, Eddy is designed around what's actually available: shared computer labs, students partnering on a single device, or a teacher running it through a projector or interactive whiteboard for the whole class."
  - title: "Safety built in, not bolted on"
    body: "Access is limited to the classroom and school hours only, accounts are distributed exclusively by teachers, and there's no path for outside contact from anyone outside the paired classrooms."

Looking ahead, we'd want to expand beyond a single city, add features for parent involvement, give students a path to eventually meet their buddies in person, run actual user testing with elementary school students instead of relying on our own past experience, and integrate self-paced learning modules to match where the broader market is heading."
