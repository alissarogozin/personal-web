reflectionBody: "This was my first data visualization project, and it taught me that the hardest work happens before you touch a chart tool: understanding messy civic data and figuring out what story it can honestly support. The flat scatter-plot trendline reshaped my entire approach, pushing me toward a more nuanced finding than my initial theory predicted, and taught me to communicate that honestly rather than overstating certainty for a cleaner narrative. Translating the Tableau dashboard into neighborhood posters also pushed me to design for a very different audience, someone passing a bus stop with five seconds to spare, not a dashboard user with five minutes. This project showed me that data visualization is as much an exercise in editorial judgment as it is in technical execution."
